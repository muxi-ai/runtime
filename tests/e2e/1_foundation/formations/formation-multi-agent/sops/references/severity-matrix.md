# Incident Severity Matrix

## P1 - Critical
- Complete service outage
- Data loss or corruption
- Security breach
- >50% of users affected
- Revenue impact >$10K/hour

**Response Time**: Immediate
**Escalation**: CEO, CTO, VP Engineering

## P2 - High
- Partial service degradation
- Key feature unavailable
- 10-50% of users affected
- Revenue impact $1K-$10K/hour

**Response Time**: Within 30 minutes
**Escalation**: VP Engineering, Team Lead

## P3 - Medium
- Minor feature issues
- <10% of users affected
- Workaround available
- Revenue impact <$1K/hour

**Response Time**: Within 2 hours
**Escalation**: Team Lead, On-call Engineer

## P4 - Low
- Cosmetic issues
- No user impact
- Internal tools affected

**Response Time**: Next business day
**Escalation**: Team backlog
