## Best Practices for Multi-step Clarification

1. **Limit Depth**: Keep clarification chains under 10 steps
2. **Clear Questions**: Each question should be unambiguous
3. **Progressive Disclosure**: Ask for required params first
4. **Validation Early**: Validate as parameters are collected
5. **Escape Routes**: Provide cancellation options
