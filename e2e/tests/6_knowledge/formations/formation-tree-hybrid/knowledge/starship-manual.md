# SS Steadfast Technical Operations Manual

This manual covers the operation and maintenance of all major subsystems of the SS Steadfast. It is the authoritative reference for the engineering crew.

## Propulsion

Overview of the propulsion system and its operating envelope. Routine inspection of this subsystem follows the standard checklist: verify seals, log pressure and temperature readings, confirm sensor calibration against the reference block, and record any deviation in the engineering journal. Operators should review the trend data weekly and escalate anomalies to the section chief. Spare parts for this subsystem are stocked in bay 4; consult the quartermaster ledger before ordering replacements. Training refreshers are mandatory every six months and cover both nominal operation and degraded-mode procedures.
