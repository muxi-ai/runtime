# Fleet Support FAQ

## What are the fleet support desk hours?

The fleet support desk is staffed from 09:00 to 17:00 UTC, Monday through Friday.

## How do I contact fleet support?

Email the support desk at support@steadfast.example or raise a ticket on the
fleet portal. Urgent hull-integrity issues should be phoned in directly.

## How long does a standard resupply request take?

Standard resupply requests are fulfilled within 5 business days. Priority
requests approved by a section chief are fulfilled within 24 hours.

## Where is the quartermaster ledger kept?

The quartermaster ledger is maintained in bay 4 and mirrored to the fleet
portal nightly.
