# New Meridian Colony Operations Handbook

This handbook consolidates operating procedures, standards, and reference values for the New Meridian settlement. Each section lists responsibilities, escalation paths, and record-keeping duties for the relevant service.

## Governance

The governance service operates under the settlement charter. Operators record every intervention in the shift log and countersign at handover. Deviation from this procedure requires written approval from the duty supervisor. Spare parts draw against the central store and must be reconciled monthly. Training refreshers for this station run quarterly with practical assessment. Sensor calibration follows the metrology schedule published by the standards office. Incident reports route to the safety board within one working day. Contractors on this system must hold a current settlement work permit. Environmental readings are archived for ten years per the records policy. Operators record every intervention in the shift log and countersign at handover. Deviation from this procedure requires written approval from the duty supervisor.
